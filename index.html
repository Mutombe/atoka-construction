FAQ

Can't find your answer? Talk to us.

How accurate is the online estimate?

The online estimate gives you an indicative range based on current Harare build rates per m². After a site visit and a look at your plans, we issue a fixed, itemised quotation — usually within five working days.

Do you help with plans and council approvals?

Yes. Our design partners prepare architectural and structural drawings, and we handle submission and inspections with the local authority.

How are payments structured?

Payments are staged against milestones — foundations, wall plate, roof, and finishes — so you only pay for work that has been completed and inspected.

Can I buy materials without hiring Atoka to build?

Absolutely. The online shop is open to everyone. Contractors can request trade quotes on larger orders straight from the cart.

Do you deliver outside Harare?

We deliver nationwide. Delivery is charged at $5 plus $0.65 per km from our Msasa yard, with a flatbed surcharge on loads over one tonne. Collection from the yard is free.
